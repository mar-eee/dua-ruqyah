Issues needing source or qualified scholarly review:

- Record 35, paragraph 7–8: the source attributes the “best of you learn and teach the Quran” report to Abu Hurairah; [Al-Bukhari 5027](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5027) displays Utsman as narrator. The Indonesian retains the source attribution pending route verification.
- Record 35, paragraph 11: a Bengali sentence appears to lack negation when discussing how many educated Muslims and scholars today memorize the Quran; the surrounding lament points in the opposite direction. The Indonesian follows the paragraph's evident argument. Correct the source before publication.
- Record 35, paragraph 37: the supplied Arabic for Az-Zumar 39:23 has `خُلُودُ` where the verse has `جُلُودُ` ([Quran Corpus 39:23:10](https://corpus.quran.com/wordmorphology.jsp?location=%2839%3A23%3A10%29)). Record 35, paragraph 48: supplied Arabic for Al-A'raf 7:205 has `وَجِيفَةً` where the verse has `وَخِيفَةً` ([Quran.com Arabic](https://quran.com/ar/al-araf/205)). Both source spans remain unchanged as required; they must be corrected in the authoritative source before publishing them as Quranic text.
- Record 35, paragraphs 59–60: the assertion that pronunciation influenced by a non-Arabic language never counts as Quran recitation is the author's interpretation, not independently established here. Record 36, paragraph 12: the claim that looking at the mushaf is a separate act of worship also needs source and scholarly review.
- Record 37, paragraphs 11–12: the source identifies the observer of the Anshari man as Abdullah bin Umar; commonly cited versions identify Abdullah bin Amr bin Al-As. Verify the exact route in Musnad Ahmad before correcting the source. Paragraph 30 includes a meaning attributed to Fussilat 41:34–35, while the supplied Arabic excerpt contains only 41:34. Paragraph 38 groups anger-management practices from multiple reports; check each wording, grade, and application separately.
- Record 38: the source presents depression and despair of divine mercy as overlapping and suggests depression is rarely physical. These are attributed views, **not** clinical facts or grounds to judge a person's faith. [Yusuf 12:87](https://quran.com/12/87) addresses despair of Allah's mercy, not a medical diagnosis. This text must not displace mental-health care or be used to stigmatize a person experiencing depression.
- Record 40, paragraphs 4–5: source labels [Ibn Majah 4102](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:4102) and [At-Tirmidzi 2465](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2465) *sahih*, while displayed Darussalam grades are *da'if*. Paragraph 7: source calls [At-Tirmidzi 2003](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2003) *sahih*, while the displayed Darussalam grade is *hasan*. The Indonesian attributes those ratings to the source; reconcile editions, chains, and grading authorities.
