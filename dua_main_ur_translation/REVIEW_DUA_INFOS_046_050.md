## Meaning and source decisions

- The long discussion of halal earnings includes broad claims about all bank products, insurance, mortgages, salaries, gifts and the acceptance of worship. Urdu retains the full claims and examples while identifying them as the author’s views, not a universal contemporary fatwa. The stated distinction between a loan repaid with an increment and a pre-agreed credit sale is preserved, as are its conditions and prices.
- Four source citation numbers do not identify the quoted reports in common modern numbering. Urdu corrects Bukhari **3993 → 7430** for the pure-earnings report ([matching report](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:7430), [source number](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3993)); Bukhari **213 → 2312** for Bilal’s date exchange ([matching report](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2312)); Bukhari **1954 → 2059** for heedlessness about halal and haram income ([matching report](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2059), [source number](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1954)); and Muslim **275 → 2735** for impatience in supplication ([matching report](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2735c)). The source values remain untouched in their fields; corrections are recorded in `GLOSSARY.json` number overrides. A separate part-by-part numeric comparison found no other changed digit values.
- The wording about purity and charity was compared with [Bukhari 7430](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:7430); the ablution/illicit-charity report with [Muslim 224](https://sunnah.com/muslim:224a); the warning on others’ rights with [Bukhari 2449](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2449) and [Muslim 2581](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2581); and enjoining good with [Tirmidhi 2169](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2169).
- The supplied Quran excerpt in 049 is preserved exactly; its meaning and reference were checked against [41:51](https://quran.com/41/51). The trade-and-usury passage was checked against [2:275](https://quran.com/2/275).
- In 049 the source asserts that Allah will forgive the affected person’s share when direct pardon is unavailable, while also insisting on divine justice and uncertainty for the wrongdoer. Urdu explicitly attributes that claim to the author. It should not be treated as a guaranteed dispensation from restitution.
- The source grades Tirmidhi 3382 as sahih, whereas the displayed modern grading is hasan ([Tirmidhi 3382](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3382)). Its attribution remains explicit. For Tirmidhi 3479, the source attributes a hasan judgment to al-Albani while a modern displayed grading says da'if ([Tirmidhi 3479](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3479)); the Urdu preserves the named attribution without resolving the grading dispute. Other secondary-book citations and older-edition page references are retained, not falsely certified.
