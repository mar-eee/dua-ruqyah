## Meaning and source decisions

- In 061 the Bengali argument for correct Arabic pronunciation appears to lose a negation in a sentence saying non-Arabic pronunciation counts as recitation, although every surrounding sentence says the opposite. Urdu follows the contextual negative sense but makes the author's categorical claim explicitly his own, not a ruling about an identifiable reader. The supplied [Quran 73:4](https://quran.com/73/4) excerpt is preserved exactly. Its source spelling `الْقُرْآَنَ` contains an extra vowel mark compared with the usual `الْقُرْآنَ`; this protected Arabic needs an upstream editorial check before publication. The verse meaning and measured-recitation theme were checked against the Quran; the [Abu Dawud 1468](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1468) voice-beautification report was checked separately.
- In 062 the meanings of [Quran 38:29](https://quran.com/38/29), [54:17](https://quran.com/54/17), [47:24](https://quran.com/47/24) and [2:121](https://quran.com/2/121) were compared with the cited verses. The Bengali expands 54:17's remembrance/admonition to include understanding; Urdu follows the verse meaning without changing Arabic. The assertion that comprehension and obedience determine whether anything counts as *tilawah* remains attributed to the author. All 13 listed recitation practices are represented; the advice about looking at mushaf pages is not certified here as an independently established Sunnah requirement.
- In 063–064 the “man of Paradise” account wrongly names Abdullah ibn Umar in Bengali. The [cited Musnad Ahmad report](https://hadithweb.com/ahmad:12697) names Abdullah ibn Amr ibn al-As, corrected in Urdu in both parts. The source's sequence, three visits, three nights, good speech, lack of envy and right-to-redress distinction are retained. Several distinct service-to-others reports are described as multiple reports, not one composite Prophetic quotation. The strong-believer passage was checked against [Muslim 2664](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2664). Counsel on despair and patience is devotional advice, not a clinical diagnosis or reason to avoid care.
- In 065 the supplied Arabic from [Quran 41:34](https://quran.com/41/34-35) ends before the source's additional meaning from 41:35. Urdu identifies the next verse separately without inserting missing Arabic. The Bengali gloss of [Quran 3:134](https://quran.com/3/134) omits “Allah loves the doers of good,” which *is* in the supplied Arabic; the Urdu meaning includes it. [Quran 42:37](https://quran.com/42/37) and the anger-control report in [Bukhari 6114](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6114) were compared with their sources. The closing list of anger-management practices remains the author's account; individual practices were not all authenticated in this batch.
